Questions

Frequently asked

♡
Do I have to be a Christian?

No. Love Matters is grounded in Scripture and modeled on how Jesus actually lived, and you are welcome here whatever your faith or background.

How much time does it take each day?

Each day has a short video from Kelly (3 to 14 minutes) and a guide with a few reflective questions, a small habit and a prayer. Write as much or as little as feels right.

What if I miss a day?

In Kelly's words: “If you happen to miss a day, it's okay. It doesn't mean you messed up.” There is no falling behind here. Move at your own pace.

Is this a challenge?

No. “This is not a challenge. It’s not something you can fail at. It’s not about fixing yourself.”

Can I do it on my phone?

Yes. Each lesson arrives by email, and the video and guide open right on your phone. You're welcome to print the guide or use your own notebook.

How long can I watch the lessons?

Your class library stays open, so you can come back to any day whenever you need it.
